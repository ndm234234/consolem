01 • ZERO INBOUND Camera + NVR behind NAT
Outbound-Only Connectivity — Unhackable from Outside
Every connection (agent reports, viewer/archive long-poll intents, live media push, and SD-card backfill to the NVR receiver) is initiated outbound by the camera itself. There are zero open RTSP/HTTP ports externally, and no site VPN is needed — only strictly typed platform commands cross the link, never raw IP traffic.
02 • BRSKI & mTLS Node Authentication
Passwordless Onboarding (RFC 8995) & Mutual TLS
Firmware images are byte-identical across units — zero shared secrets in firmware. Devices join the domain using factory IDevID / TPM 2.0 and a signed voucher (or console approval), receive a customer-CA LDevID, and permanently wipe bootstrap credentials. All inter-service calls use role-verified mTLS.
03 • E2E SEALING At Rest & In Transit
End-to-End Secret Sealing & Zero-Knowledge Relay
Every *_secret field on camera flash and NVR disks is sealed by the keyring (enc:v1) with AAD bound to the exact row path and JSON Pointer (copying ciphertext to another row fails decryption). In transit, config books are sealed to the recipient’s X25519 key — relays and peer nodes cannot read stream tokens.
04 • URL & CREDENTIAL HYGIENE Leak Prevention
Architectural Ban on Credentials in URLs & Logs
NVR and camera doors reject at ingress (HTTP 400) any URL containing embedded usernames, passwords, or secret query parameters (rtsp://user:pass@…, ?token=…) before it ever touches storage or pending queues. State snapshots (snapshot), APIs, and logs automatically strip *_secret fields.
05 • LEAST PRIVILEGE BY SPEC Inside NVR & Server
Process Isolation, Role Unix Sockets & Signer Enclave
Storage permissions (configstore-rights.json) are generated directly from the declarative spec: each service connects over its own role Unix socket and sees only its key prefixes. Domain private keys live exclusively inside w2c-domain (signer) — neither the console nor VMS workers ever touch private keys, requesting narrow 24h tokens (stream/ask) over a local socket.
06 • OFFLINE REVOCATION & SURVIVAL Resilience & Audit
Offline Access Revocation & Disconnected Survival
Tokens carry only subject identity and expiry, while permissions are checked locally against replicated grants and the domain/revoked list; short lifetimes guarantee a bounded revocation window even offline. If cut off from the domain, NVRs and cameras continue recording, enforcing permissions, and logging local audit trails.
The w2c Difference: cameras and NVRs run the exact same platform engine — eliminating legacy ONVIF/RTSP passwords, open inbound ports, and hardcoded firmware keys.
