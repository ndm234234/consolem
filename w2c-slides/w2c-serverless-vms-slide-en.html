01 • CLUSTER OF ONE Node Architecture
Every Camera Is a Full Cluster of One — Not a “Dumb Stream”
The camera runs the same platform engine as a datacenter node: its own console, epoch, local grant verifier, and SD archive. Intra-camera consensus comes for free (one flash — one writer), eliminating any need for distributed Raft across cameras over lossy PoE or wireless links.
02 • ZERO-SERVER DOMAIN Fault-Tolerant Control Plane
Domain Holder on a Camera with Instant Term Failover
Domain services run directly on one of the site’s cameras, while a signed and encrypted backup (domain/backup-taken) is automatically replicated by agents to designated keeper cameras. If the holder camera is unplugged or stolen, any peer camera assumes the domain at term + 1 — preserving all settings, grants, and pending queues.
03 • EDGE + NETWORK STORAGE Hybrid Archive
Network Storages & SD Cards as One Self-Healing Archive
Each camera records into its local 32 MiB RAM ring and SD card segments while streaming to network storage volumes. When the network or NAS drops, recording never stops: once connectivity returns, the reconciliation loop (desired − actual) backfills only the exact missing intervals from the camera’s SD card.
04 • SCALE TO HUNDREDS Fleet Scalability
Hundreds of Cameras & Dozens Offline with Zero Degradation
A camera’s domain contract is just ~600 bytes (heartbeats + snapshot). Parallel polling lanes (lanes=16), in-memory ReadView.where lookups in 0 network hops, and exponential backoff for silent nodes complete a 300-camera sweep (with 30 offline) in 1.4 seconds.
05 • FLASH & RAM DISCIPLINE Embedded Linux Footprint
Strict 40 MiB RAM Ceiling & Zero Idle Flash Wear
No heavy database engine runs on the camera: frame memory is strictly partitioned (32 MiB ring, 6 MiB card queue, 2×1 MiB push buffers — 33.7 MiB during peak catch-up). Heartbeats and freshness timestamps stay in RAM; stream keys rotate at half-life (every 12h), and unchanged backups perform zero flash writes.
06 • OFFLINE EDITS & HONEST VIEW Field Operations
Pending Edits for Powered-Off Cameras & Honest Directory
Operators can update camera settings or access rights even while cameras are powered down: the domain stores changes in domain/pending/<member>, and the camera’s agent pulls and applies them upon boot. The directory never confuses “empty data” with “device unreachable,” explicitly surfacing report age.
Deployment Economics: scale seamlessly from 1 standalone camera with an SD card to a distributed mesh of hundreds of cameras, relays, and network volumes — with zero dedicated VMS servers.
